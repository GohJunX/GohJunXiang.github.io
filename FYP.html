01
Jobseeker & Employer Platform
Web Application Interface

A platform designed to simplify the job search process.

I developed the Weblog for Jobseekers and Employers as my Final Year Project. The system was created to provide a platform that connects jobseekers with employers.

Jobseekers can browse and filter available job opportunities, submit applications and receive notifications. Employers can register, verify their accounts and create and manage job postings.

The project also includes additional features such as online quizzes, interview scheduling, notifications, blog content, short videos and an AI chatbot to improve the overall user experience.
